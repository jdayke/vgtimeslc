# VGTimes and LibertyCity Shorts rules

## Outlet routing

- Publish general gaming topics as VGTimes.
- Use LibertyCity only for Rockstar Games topics such as GTA, Red Dead Redemption, and closely related Rockstar material.
- Never carry VGTimes subtitle colors or overlays into a LibertyCity version. Use the supplied or previously approved LibertyCity style.

## Canvas and safe zones

- Master canvas: 1080x1920.
- Keep critical text and faces inside approximately `x=70..940` and `y=250..1550`.
- Reserve at least 120 px on the right for platform controls and about 300 px at the bottom for captions and UI.
- Keep feed-cover hooks inside the centered 1080x1440 crop, approximately `y=240..1680`.
- Do not place the hook against the top edge. The user's Instagram grid reference is the authority when provided.
- Use compact hook backgrounds. Do not create a large field of solid fill around short text.

## X-repost layout

For the approved 1080x1920 X-style repost template:

- Use a light card about 610 px tall.
- Leave the first 250–270 px mostly empty.
- Begin the avatar/name block near `y=255..275`.
- Begin post copy near `y=405..430`.
- Keep the left margin near 64–70 px and the right margin at least 120 px.
- Start the video near `y=610`.
- For a wide source, first fit it to 1080 px width, then enlarge both dimensions to 125% and center it horizontally. This normally crops 135 px from each side of a 16:9 source.
- Inspect at least six representative frames. Fall back to the original 100% fit-to-width size only when the default crop substantially removes a face, main action, essential HUD, captions, or another important subject.
- Keep the unused lower canvas light like the card. Never stretch the source or crop it to fill the full vertical remainder.
- Use the clean VGTimes round red mark, verified badge, `VGTimes`, and `@VGTimes`.
- For LibertyCity repost cards, crop the transparent margins from the original blue LC emblem in `D:\VGTimes\Shorts\LC 003 RIGHT.png` and fit it in the avatar slot. Set the display name to `LibertyCity` with the verified badge and the handle to `@libertycitynet` in lowercase.

## Hooks and subtitles

- Write all added hooks, labels, and subtitles in English. Translate non-English user copy into natural English before rendering.
- The opening hook may differ from the first spoken sentence.
- After the hook, subtitles must match the spoken words exactly and follow their timing.
- Use ALL CAPS unless the user requests another style.
- Prefer one line; use two lines only when necessary for readability.
- Do not put a period at the end of a subtitle card. Periods inside a subtitle remain valid.
- Avoid sharpen, heavy outlines, halos, noisy shadows, and background artifacts around letters.
- Keep subtitle cards within safe zones and verify the longest line in a rendered frame.

## Editing quality

- Never allow fully black technical frames.
- Inspect every cut around the edit point; remove stray one-frame flashes and leftover transitions.
- Do not repeat the same shot three times. Repeat twice only when the repetition has an intentional comic or narrative purpose.
- Let shots breathe. When uncertain, make plans about 0.3–0.5 seconds longer instead of hypercutting.
- Start with a clear, centered, visually strong subject. Do not hide the key character in a corner.
- Preserve complete actions: if a shot starts a throw, hit, jump, or reveal, show the payoff unless the cut is intentionally motivated.
- Use enough shot variety to sustain attention throughout the Short.

## Branding and assets

- Use only the current user-approved VGTimes or LibertyCity overlay asset. Do not substitute an older logo or template from memory.
- Do not add `ARCHIVE FOOTAGE` or similar labels unless the user or factual context requires them.
- Keep mod/fan-made/unused-audio labels accurate. Do not present fan footage as official gameplay.

## Delivery defaults

- Use original source audio unless VO, music, or silence is requested.
- Prefer H.264/AAC MP4 with `faststart`.
- Use NVENC on the RTX 4090 when available.
- Deliver previews and finals under clear versioned names; do not overwrite a previously approved export.
