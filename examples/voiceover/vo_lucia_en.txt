GTA 6's second main character just got out of prison.
Lucia Caminos was taught to fight by her father when she was still a kid.
Years later, fighting for her family landed her inside Leonida Penitentiary.
She eventually got out...
and now she wants the kind of life her mother always dreamed of.
Then she met Jason.
And together, they're about to turn one bad decision into something much bigger.
